# Management report standard (RU)

Audience: management of a commercial bank. Default output: restrained analytical note, not research log, pitch deck or an AI self-evaluation.

- Use **Times New Roman**, A4, 10.5–12 pt main text, moderate margins, numbered sections, concise informative headings, simple tables with explicit units and dates.
- Opening paragraph: what was established and what action/decision is proposed, with confidence and conditions.
- Then scope/definitions, proven comparable cases, market numbers, legal/economic conditions, recommendations and primary sources.
- Every material number near its source or source-number reference. Preserve clickable URLs in source section.
- Do not include tokens such as `judge`, `agent benchmark`, `AI-generated`, `sprint`, `prompt`, `research pipeline`, numeric scoring, version change log, system messages or speculative claims about reasons for a specific bank's balance unless the evidence directly supports them.
- Prefer «межбанковский депозит» to «interbank deposit», «совокупная стоимость» to «all-in», «посредник» to «introducer», «отбор документов» to «evidence», «проверка» to «gate»; retain established product names (RUONIA, BIS, SWIFT, ALCO, РЕПО) when translating would obscure meaning.
- Avoid decorative badges, colored callouts and page-filling covers. Make source index readable in print.
- Never say a historical tariff is a current market price; court case fees are disputed expenses unless judgment proves payment.
- Render each DOCX page and inspect visually for clipping, table wrapping, small type and source links. Keep only final user-requested files for management.
