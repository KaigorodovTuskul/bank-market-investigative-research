# Russian banking legal/tax verification matrix

Research checkpoints, not pre-approved legal positions. Recheck controlling legal edition for the transaction date and product; use primary official law where possible. For unavailable complete texts state the limitation and request certified/current documents.

- **Banking authority:** Russian Bank Law 395-1 Art. 28 on interbank operations; participant legal status, representation and regulated brokerage if relevant.
- **Agent contract:** Civil Code Arts. 1005–1008; mandate, documented introduction, event triggering fee, reporting and whether acting in own or principal's name.
- **AML/CTF:** 115-FZ; identification, beneficial ownership, foreign bank status, representatives, sanctions/restrictions, record keeping. Never suggest circumvention.
- **Cross-border:** 173-FZ, currency and contractual restrictions; foreign governing law, counterparty legal capacity, permitted settlement bank and route.
- **Tax:** Tax Code Arts. 252 and 291, as applicable, for banking expense substantiation; VAT place-of-supply and tax-agent issues (Arts. 148 and 161) and foreign-income withholding as relevant. Do not infer a fixed tax result from historical court cases.
- **Banking risk:** counterparty exposure, funding concentration, maturity, LCR/NSFR equivalents and Russian mandatory prudential ratios if applicable, reserves, liability enforceability, FX hedging.

Use a decision matrix for every proposed route: permitted / potentially feasible pending legal opinion / unacceptably risky / insufficient evidence. Record precise condition and responsible reviewer. Do not claim that the agent can hold principal cash without a separate legal basis.
