# Source discovery and search

1. Define *exact transaction*: who lends, who borrows, asset class, direct legal contract, agent actions and compensation.
2. Search official primary sources: Central Bank, MOEX/market rules, legislation, arbitration case registry, official bank disclosures, BIS international banking statistics.
3. Search RU legal variants: `агентский договор банк привлечения депозитов`, `посредник межбанковский депозит`, `мандатное письмо финансирование банка`, `вознаграждение за привлечение средств банк`, specific case number plus original court, `инвестиционный брокер денежный рынок`.
4. Search EN equivalents: `interbank deposit broker`, `money market deposit brokerage`, `institutional cash deposits`, `bank funding intermediary`, `introducing broker deposits`, `cross-border bank deposits brokerage`.
5. Screen each candidate: court original > reproduction; the subject of the transaction is a bank or corporation? Is an actual funding flow documented? Is the agent independent? Are execution and tariff supported?
6. Use country- or language-specific search only when justified; do not expand generically into retail referrals, credit cards or corporate-account acquisition if the decision concerns bank-to-bank funding.
7. Preserve positive and negative evidence. Prioritize a new source only if it can change the decision or confidence.

## Fallback reading

Try original URL → browser extraction → `r.jina.ai/https://domain/path` as text reader → search-indexed excerpt. Revisit the original and save the final exact URL. Rendered text is not by itself legal authenticity.
