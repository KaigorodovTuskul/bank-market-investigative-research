# Bank market investigation — minimal fallback

If the task involves banking market investigation, source-backed interbank/funding research or executive reports, read `skills/bank-market-investigative-research/SKILL.md` when relevant. Do not preload its references. Distinguish source evidence from inference, balances from flows, and bank-to-bank funding from corporate deposits.
